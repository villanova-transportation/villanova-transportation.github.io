MOVEMENT IS HUMAN

Better data.
Deeper understanding.
Meaningful change.

How we move shapes access to opportunity, exposure to climate risks, and the spread of disease. Understanding those connections is at the heart of our work.

Led by Dr. Chenfeng Xiong, Villanova Human Mobility Labs brings together transportation engineering, data science, and public health. We turn complex mobility patterns into evidence that communities and decision-makers can use.

High school and undergraduate students are welcome to join our research community through VSRI. Work with our team on mobility data, AI, transportation, climate, health, and equity.

High school juniors and seniors can apply for an 8–10 week full-time summer experience. Undergraduates can also explore year-round research opportunities.
